How it runs

The process, in Six stages.

Phase 01

Discovery & Scoping

Understanding business needs, constraints, and data availability. We scope the real problem and define what "done" means before writing any code.

Phase 02

Architecture & Design

System blueprinting and selecting the right LLMs, databases, and multi-agent frameworks. This is the foundation that prevents expensive re-architecting later.

Phase 03

Data Engineering & RAG

Building the nervous system: scraping, cleaning, vectorizing documents, and setting up high-performance Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines.

Phase 04

Model Integration & Tuning

Crafting robust system prompts, fine-tuning behavior, and establishing evaluation metrics to ensure the AI behaves predictably and accurately.

Phase 05

Full-Stack App Build

Developing the user-facing interfaces and secure backend APIs. Shipped in working increments so you can review a real product, not just a slide deck.

Phase 06

Deployment & Monitoring

Shipping to production, configuring automated CI/CD pipelines, and setting up telemetry for real-time observability of the agent systems.
